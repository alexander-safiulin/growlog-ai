CANNA Aqua Vega

Aqua Vega is a professional fast working nutrient for plants containing all the essential elements for optimal growing. Aqua Vega is designed for growing in recirculating hydroponic systems such as NFT, ebb and flow systems and the cultivation on clay pellets.
