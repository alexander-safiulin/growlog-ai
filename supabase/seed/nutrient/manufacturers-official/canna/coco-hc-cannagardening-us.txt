CANNA COCO HC A&B

CANNA Coco HC A&B fertilizers are a high concentrate version of the well-loved CANNA COCO fertilizers, designed for growing on all coco substrates. 

CANNA Coco HC A&B is composed of the highest quality source materials and provides all that plants need for optimal growing and flowering. The fertilizer is designed with the right blend of nutrients for fast growing plants. 

The HC solution for the professional market offers a significant economical saving without compromising ease and quality control.

Image
ONE FORMULA DESIGNED FOR GROWTH AND BLOOM

Thanks to CANNA Coco medium's unique buffering qualities and the formulation of CANNA Coco HC A&B fertilizers, there is no need for a separate formulation for both bloom and growth. The fertilizer is made to pair perfectly with the coco substrate.

EASY TO USE, NO ADDITIONAL LABOR TO PREPARE

As a liquid nutrient, there is no need to prepare a stock concentrate from a dry soluble material. The CANNA Coco HC A&B is your stock solution – saving time, energy, and giving you peace of mind knowing exactly what is in your fertilizer solution.

SUITABLE FOR ALL IRRIGATION SYSTEMS

CANNA Coco HC A&B is suitable for all watering systems, including injection and reservoir based systems.

COST SAVING BENEFITS

By producing these products at higher concentrated levels, the CANNA Coco HC A&B line is convenient for professional use. Since only half the amount of concentrate is needed for the same nutrient solution and cultivation results, transport, stock and handling costs are significantly reduced.

Directions Of Use

CANNA Coco HC A&B is intended to be used on indoor or outdoor crop production and gardening. 

Shake well before use.
A ready to use fertilizer solution is made by diluting A&B concentrates in equal amounts with water.
Mix at the maximum rate of 20 ml of concentrate per 10 liters of water.
Do not pre-mix concentrated nutrients.
Store container in a dark area, above 40°F, avoiding extreme cold and hot environments.
