CANNA TERRA
CANNA Terra Flores

CANNA Terra Flores is a one-part nutrient for plants during the flowering stage. It contains the right amount of potassium and phosphorus to encourage fruit development and help each plant achieve its characteristic flavor. It is suitable for growing in pots, open soil, and both in and outdoors.
