what is pH Perfect® Grow, Micro, Bloom?

Our Ph.D. plant scientists designed pH Perfect® Grow, Micro, Bloom with simplicity in mind. This three-part, next-generation base solution keeps the pH levels of a high-value crop in the ideal range, while delivering a wide range of bioavailable nutrients to produce bountiful harvests.

  • Optimal ratios of micro, secondary, and macronutrients unlock plant potential

What is pH Perfect® Grow, Micro, Bloom?

This is a three-part base nutrient system designed by our Ph.D. plant scientists. It provides optimal ratios of macro, micro, and secondary nutrients to produce bountiful harvests. It features proprietary pH Perfect® Technology, which automatically keeps your pH levels in the ideal range.

How does pH Perfect® Technology benefit my grow?

Our patented technology creates a “sweet spot” for your pH and holds it there for one week. This safeguards your plants from sudden pH fluctuations and ensures maximum nutrient uptake. Using this system allows you to:

  • Eliminate pH pens and meters: It is the single most effective way to stop manually adjusting pH levels.
  • Maximize Absorption: A balanced pH ensures your plants absorb the micro, macro, and secondary nutrients they need to thrive.
  • Save Time: You are freed from the tedious details of pH adjustment.
What is the correct order for mixing the three components?

The mixing order is critical for the product to work correctly. You must follow this specific sequence:

  1. Add pH Perfect® Micro to your reservoir first and mix well.
  2. Add pH Perfect® Grow next.
  3. Add pH Perfect® Bloom last.
Can I mix the three parts together in a concentrate form to save time?

No. Do not mix the nutrients together as a concentrate. You must add them to your water reservoir individually in the correct order (Micro, then Grow, then Bloom).

Do I need to shake the bottles before use?

Yes. Nutrient components can settle at the bottom of the bottle over time. Shaking ensures you are pouring a balanced solution into your reservoir.

What feeding ratio should I use?

Extensive research on plant tissue samples indicates that plants perform best when pH Perfect® Grow, Micro, Bloom is applied in a 1:1:1 ratio.

  • Vegetative Phase: Scale from 1 mL/L up to 4 mL/L by the end of the phase.
  • Bloom Phase: Use 4 mL/L throughout the bloom phase until the flush.
Why are the liquids different colors?

The three parts are dyed specific colors using absolutely safe dyes. This helps you easily distinguish between Grow, Micro, and Bloom to avoid confusion or accidental duplication during mixing.

Why Should I Use Advanced Nutrients Products At A Lesser Strength Than General Hydroponics?

Advanced Nutrients pH Perfect® GMB is manufactured with the latest coatings, chelates and processes, which means much more nutrition is absorbed by plants. That’s why you use a lot less of our nutrients when compared to General Hydroponics, which is 25% weaker than our 3-Part.

What growing media and systems is this product compatible with?

pH Perfect® Grow, Micro, Bloom is designed for use with all diverse hydroponic growing media and continuous liquid-feed systems. This includes aeroponics, drip irrigation, NFT (Nutrient Film Technique), flood and drain, deep water culture (DWC), and is safe for coco coir.

Where can I find a feeding chart for my specific crop?

You can use the Nutrient Calculator, download one of our ready-to-use charts, or use the BudLabs app to generate a custom nutrient schedule.
